Privacy Policy

for Magic Number Merge

Compliant with GDPR Compliant with CCPA App Store Ready Google Play Ready

Last updated: May 18, 2026 | Effective date: May 18, 2026

Welcome to Magic Number Merge (referred to as "the Game"). We take your privacy and the security of your personal information very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile game.

🔒 Our Core Principles:
  • We do NOT collect unnecessary personal information
  • We do NOT sell or trade your personal information
  • We only use data when necessary to improve your gaming experience
  • You can request deletion of your data at any time

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

1.1 Game Progress Data

  • Level progress and completion status
  • In-game scores, achievements, and statistics
  • Game settings and preference configurations
  • Important: This data is stored locally on your device only and is not uploaded to our servers.

1.2 Device Information

  • Device model and operating system version
  • Application version number
  • Language and region settings
  • This information is used solely for optimizing game performance and compatibility

1.3 Advertising Services

This game uses third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Pangle). These services may collect:

  • Anonymous advertising identifiers (IDFA for iOS, GAID for Android)
  • Ad impression and click data
  • Approximate geographic location for relevant ad targeting

This information is governed by the privacy policies of our third-party advertising partners.

1.4 Analytics Services

We may use anonymous analytics tools to improve the game experience, including:

  • Game crash reports and performance metrics
  • Feature usage statistics
  • App performance and stability data

All analytics data is anonymous and not linked to your personal identity.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • ✅ Providing and maintaining core game functionality
  • ✅ Improving the gaming experience and user interface
  • ✅ Fixing bugs and optimizing performance
  • ✅ Developing new features and content
  • ✅ Personalizing your gaming experience
  • ✅ Sending important game update notifications

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Game progress data is stored locally on your device, protected by system backup mechanisms
  • We do not upload your personal game data to cloud servers
  • We use industry-standard security measures to protect any limited data we may collect
  • All communications with third-party services use encrypted transmission

4. Third-Party Services

This game may include the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Advertising Services: Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Pangle
  • Analytics Services: Unity Analytics, Firebase Analytics
  • App Stores: Apple App Store, Google Play Store

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party services to understand how they handle your information.

5. Children's Privacy

Our game is intended for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete that information.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: You can request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification: You can request correction of inaccurate information
  • Right to Erasure: You can request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can request restriction of processing your data
  • Right to Object: You can object to processing of your personal data
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

7. Uninstalling the Game

When you uninstall the game, all locally stored game data will be deleted from your device. To stop all data collection, simply uninstall the game from your device.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you through in-game notification or other appropriate means. Your continued use of the game after the effective date of the updated policy constitutes your acceptance of the changes.

9. Consent

By downloading, installing, and using this game, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not install or use the game.

10. Data Retention

We retain any collected data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. Anonymous analytics data may be retained for up to 24 months for statistical purposes.
