SiteMiles is a mileage log for people who drive for work. You can start or stop a trip, add a past trip by hand, and mark trips as business or personal. With a SiteMiles Premium subscription, you can also export a monthly report and use other paid features described below.

Logging trips

Manual logging is always available, including if you decline location access or later turn location off in iOS Settings.

Optional automatic trip detection can be turned on in Settings. It may miss trips and is not guaranteed to record every trip.

Mileage rate estimates

The mileage rate in Settings is an estimate you can change. Amounts shown in the app are calculated from the rate you set. They are not tax advice and do not guarantee a deduction or reimbursement. SiteMiles does not replace a tax, legal, or accounting professional.

What is included with SiteMiles Premium

These features require a SiteMiles Premium subscription:

  • Unlimited automatic trips
  • Full-period totals and monthly or date-range summaries
  • CSV and PDF export
  • Custom purposes

Unlimited manual logging and business or personal classification are available without a subscription. Automatic trip detection, if you use it, is limited unless you subscribe.

Subscriptions and billing

SiteMiles Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple In-App Purchase:

  • $4.99 per month
  • $49.99 per year, with a 7-day free trial

Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Unused time in a free trial is forfeited when you purchase.

To cancel or manage your subscription, open iOS Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. You can also use the manage-subscription path shown in the app. Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple’s refund policies.

A canceled, failed, or refunded purchase does not keep paid features unlocked. You can restore a valid purchase with Restore Purchases in the app.
