Privacy Policy

for Black Hole Blocks

Compliant with GDPR Compliant with CCPA App Store Ready

Last updated: October 15, 2026 | Effective date: October 15, 2026

Welcome to Black Hole Blocks (referred to as "the Game"), a casual 3D sliding puzzle game published by Nanjing Weilu Culture Media Co., Ltd. ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you play the Game and how you can control it.

🔒 At a Glance:
  • No account, login, or registration is required
  • There are no in-app purchases
  • Your game progress stays on your device; we do not run our own servers for the Game
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device data as described below
  • We do NOT sell your personal information

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Game Progress and Settings

  • Unlocked levels, completed levels, and hint usage
  • Settings such as music, sound effects, and vibration
  • Important: This data is stored locally on your device only. We do not upload it to any server.

1.2 Information Collected by Google AdMob

The Game is free and supported by ads (rewarded video ads that unlock hints, and interstitial ads between levels), served by Google AdMob. When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as the IDFA, only if you allow tracking, and the IDFV)
  • Ad interaction data (ad impressions, clicks, and rewarded ad completions)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language, app version)
  • Diagnostic data (crash and performance data related to the ads SDK)

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

2. Consent and Your Choices

  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS, the Game asks for your permission before your advertising identifier (IDFA) can be used to track you across apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and you will still see ads, which may be less relevant. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Google UMP consent: In regions where required (such as the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, and certain U.S. states), the Game shows a consent message powered by Google's User Messaging Platform so you can accept or decline personalized ads. You can revisit your choice through the privacy option provided in the Game.
  • Non-personalized ads: If you do not consent, Google may still serve non-personalized ads, which rely on contextual information.

3. How Information Is Used

  • ✅ Saving your progress and settings on your device
  • ✅ Serving and measuring ads, including rewarded hints
  • ✅ Limiting how often ads are shown, and preventing fraud
  • ✅ Keeping the Game stable and compatible with your device

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from partner apps
  • Apple App Store (distribution)

We do not use any other analytics, attribution, or social SDKs in the Game.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region), and we do not use ads targeted at children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information, to object to processing, and to opt out of personalized advertising. Because the Game does not store your data on our servers, most requests are best handled by changing your ATT and consent settings, or by contacting Google about data it holds. You may also contact us using the details below.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress remains on your device until you delete the Game. Uninstalling the Game removes all locally stored game data. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

8. Security

Communications between the Game and the ad service are encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Game after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
