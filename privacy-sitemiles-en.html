Last updated: November 26, 2026

SiteMiles is a mileage log for people who drive for work. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

  • No account, login, or registration is required.
  • Your trip log and settings stay on your device. This version of the app does not use our own cloud service.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple.
  • Location access is optional. You can log trips manually if you decline it.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs, and the app does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following on your device:

  • Trip records you create or record, such as times, distances, start and end points or addresses you enter, categories (for example, business or personal), and purposes.
  • Settings you choose, including the mileage rate estimate you set.
  • Whether you have an active SiteMiles Premium subscription, based on entitlement information provided by Apple.

This information stays on your device unless you export a report. Export creates a CSV or PDF file that you can save or share yourself. We do not receive a copy of exported files.

Deleting the app removes the locally stored trip log and settings from that device. We do not keep a server-side copy.

2. Location

Location is used only to help record trips. It is not required to use the app.

  • If you start a trip and choose to use location, the app may ask for Location When In Use so it can record distance and a route while the trip is in progress.
  • Optional automatic trip detection, if you turn it on, may ask for Location Always after an in-app explanation. This option is off by default.
  • You can decline location access or turn it off later in iOS Settings. Manual logging remains available.
  • Location used for trips is stored on your device as part of your trip log. We do not use location for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

Automatic trip detection may not record every trip. The app does not promise complete background logging.

3. Purchases

If you subscribe to SiteMiles Premium, payment is processed by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card number. Apple provides the app with your subscription status so it can unlock paid features. Restore purchases uses Apple’s official In-App Purchase system. You manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID subscription settings.

For how Apple handles payment information, see Apple’s privacy policy.

4. How information is used

  • To show your trip log, totals, and reports on your device.
  • To estimate amounts using the mileage rate you set. This is an estimate you control. It is not tax advice, and it does not guarantee a deduction or reimbursement.
  • To provide paid features if you have an active subscription.
  • To record a trip when you have allowed location access.

5. Third parties

The app uses Apple services for distribution and In-App Purchase, including subscription management. This version of the app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

6. Children

SiteMiles is intended for adults who drive for work. It is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the equivalent minimum age in your region.

7. Your choices and requests

Because trip data is stored on your device, you can delete it by deleting trips in the app or by uninstalling the app. You can change or revoke location permission in iOS Settings.

If you have a privacy question or request, contact us at the email below. We will respond within 15 business days.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information stored by the app on your device. No method of storage is completely secure. This version of the app does not transmit your trip log to our servers.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.
